How the budget is worked out

There's no fixed rule for a couple + single sharing a 2-bed. The common options are: split by room (50/50), by space (~60/40 couple/single), or by head (⅔ / ⅓). This map uses the middle ground: rent 60/40, with bills split per head (⅓ each, since they scale with people).

Where bills aren't included, they're estimated at £370/month for the flat (council tax ~£150, energy ~£130, water ~£45, broadband ~£30, TV licence ~£15). "Bills included" on a listing may not cover council tax — check.

"Only fits if split per head" means the single's ⅓ share is ≤ ~£1,000 but their 40% share isn't.

Sources: OpenRent and Rightmove (updated: ). Refreshed daily; listings go fast. Rightmove doesn't flag bills, so it's read from the listing text; most don't mention bills.
